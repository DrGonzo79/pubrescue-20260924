# Office Hours diagnostic

## Customer and painful status quo
Operations leaders at 20–300 person schools, churches, and associations have years of `.pub` newsletters, programs, handbooks, and yearbooks scattered across drives. They lack a trustworthy inventory, conversion QA, and accountable owner before the software deadline.

## Desperate use case
“Before Publisher disappears, prove every recurring or legally useful document can still be opened, printed, edited, and found—without silently breaking fonts, images, or pagination.”

## Narrowest paid wedge
A fixed-fee **Publisher Rescue Audit**: scan/export an inventory, classify layout risk, convert a representative five-file pilot to archival PDF, and deliver an exception report. Price discovery starts at $299 for up to 100 files; conversion is quoted only after the pilot.

## Observation and validation
Interview ten school office managers and ten church administrators; watch each locate and open five historical files; collect file counts and failure classes; sell three paid audits before automating conversion. Track time-to-inventory, pilot fidelity acceptance, quote acceptance, exception rate, and expansion requests.

## Future-fit
If paid audits reveal durable demand, expand to document-retention maps, accessibility remediation, reusable template rebuilding, and Microsoft 365 governance. Do not start as a generic file converter.
